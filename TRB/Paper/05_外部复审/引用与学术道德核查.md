# 二、问题清单

## 1. 全文作者—年份引用缺逗号

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：全文；例如 §1：

> “Collision avoidance for autonomous surface vessels must therefore satisfy the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea **(IMO 1972)**.”

**问题**：本文统一使用 `(Author Year)`，与本次明确要求的 TRB `(Author, Year)` 格式不一致。TRB 指南示例也使用作者与年份之间的逗号。

**改法**：全文统一改为：

- `(IMO, 1972)`
- `(Krasowski and Althoff, 2024)`
- `(Meyer et al., 2020; Heiberg et al., 2022; Müller et al., 2026)`

---

## 2. 叙述式引用被错误地写成介词加括号

**严重度：建议改**

**位置**：§4.3.1：

> “The five terms are taken **from (Krasowski and Althoff 2024)**, with three declared deviations.”

**问题**：`from (Author, Year)` 不是规范的叙述式引用结构，作者名在语法上应当进入句子。

**改法**：

> “The five terms are adapted from **Krasowski and Althoff (2024)**, with three declared deviations.”

---

## 3. CommonOcean 框架与本文所用 2,000 个场景的来源被混在一起

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：

§2.2：

> “That work provides **the scenario library** and the state machine thresholds used here.”

§5.1：

> “The experiments use the two-vessel encounter library of the CommonOcean benchmark **(Krasowski and Althoff 2022), which holds 2,000 scenarios**.”

**问题**：Krasowski and Althoff 2022 提供的是 CommonOcean 基准框架、模型和场景表示；本文所用的 2,000 个随机遭遇场景及 70/30 划分是在 Krasowski and Althoff 2024 中构造和报告的。当前 §5.1 把具体场景集归给了 2022 年框架论文。

**改法**：

> “The experiments use 2,000 two-vessel scenarios constructed by **Krasowski and Althoff (2024)** within the CommonOcean benchmark framework **(Krasowski and Althoff, 2022)**.”

§2.2 可相应改为：

> “Krasowski and Althoff (2022) provide the CommonOcean benchmark framework, while Krasowski and Althoff (2024) provide the 2,000-scenario instantiation and the state-machine thresholds used here.”

---

## 4. 致谢对场景库提供方的归属与正文不一致

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：Acknowledgements：

> “The authors thank the CommonOcean benchmark and **(Krasowski and Althoff 2024) for the public scenario library and the formalization of the rules**.”

**问题**：句子没有区分 CommonOcean 框架、2,000 个具体场景和规则形式化三个对象，也没有正式引用 CommonOcean 2022；同时 `(Krasowski...)` 被直接接在 `and` 后，语法和引文格式均不规范。

**改法**：

> “The authors thank the CommonOcean project **(Krasowski and Althoff, 2022)** for the benchmark framework and **Krasowski and Althoff (2024)** for the public 2,000-scenario instantiation and the temporal-logic formalization of the maritime rules.”

---

## 5. “三倍船长”阈值没有准确说明其在原文中的对象

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：§4.1：

> “The occupancy of the target vessel is inflated by **three times its length**, and the check horizon is 420 s.”

**问题**：Krasowski and Althoff 2024 中的精确定义是碰撞锥半径采用 \(r_m=3l_m\)，其几何后果是检测小于约两个目标船长净间距的轨迹；当前写法把它笼统描述成“目标船占用区膨胀三倍船长”，容易与该文其他安全距离参数混淆。

**改法**：

> “For the collision-possibility predicate, the collision-cone radius is set to \(r_m=3l_m\), following Krasowski and Althoff (2024, Appendix A); this corresponds to detecting trajectories with less than approximately two target-vessel lengths of clearance.”

---

## 6. 将 20°/40 s 写成不可降低的普遍合规下限，引用支撑不足

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：§4.1：

> “With a window of 40 s and a threshold of 20°...”  
> “This is the smallest compliant turn rate. Any turn at least this large complies, and the bound cannot be lowered.”

**问题**：20°和 40 s 是所采用规则形式化中的定量阈值，不是 IMO COLREGs 直接规定的普遍法定最小值；现有引用不能支撑“任何达到该值的转向都合规”以及“该下限不可降低”的无条件结论。

**改法**：

> “Under the formalization adopted from Krasowski and Althoff (2024), the minimum turn rate satisfying the 20°-within-40-s threshold is \(0.008727\ \mathrm{rad/s}\). Any turn rate at least this large satisfies **that formalized threshold**.”

删除：

> “and the bound cannot be lowered”

或限定为：

> “For the adopted 20°-within-40-s threshold, this numerical bound cannot be lowered.”

---

## 7. “被列为未来工作”的事实引错了文献

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：§2.2：

> “Action projection under continuous control is listed there as future work **(Kochdumper et al. 2023)**.”

**问题**：把连续动作修正列为未来工作的是 Krasowski and Althoff 2024；Kochdumper et al. 2023 是被其列举的动作投影方案，不是作出该未来工作陈述的来源。

**改法**：

> “Krasowski and Althoff (2024) identify continuous-action correction as future work and cite action projection as one possible mechanism **(Kochdumper et al., 2023)**.”

---

## 8. De Lellis et al. 2024 不能支撑“线性核 + 到达终止条件”

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：§2.2：

> “A linear kernel with arrival as a terminal condition is used instead **(De Lellis et al. 2024)**.”

**问题**：该文讨论的是通过奖励塑形编码 settling time、steady-state error 等控制要求；检索正文未发现 `linear kernel`、`arrival` 或将 arrival 设为 terminal condition 的对应贡献。当前句子把本文自己的奖励实现细节归到了该文。

**改法**：将实现选择与相关工作分开：

> “We use a linear distance kernel for the approach term, with arrival treated as a terminal condition. This is an implementation choice of the present work.”

若保留该文，只能另写其实际支撑的内容：

> “Reward shaping can be designed to encode control requirements such as settling time and steady-state error **(De Lellis et al., 2024)**.”

---

## 9. Markgraf et al. 2026 不能支撑“凸可达集近似和 QP”的具体归属

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：§2.2：

> “The reachable set is represented by a convex over-approximation and the correction by a quadratic program **(Markgraf et al. 2026)**, rather than by a higher-order set representation, which keeps the per-step cost small.”

**问题**：Markgraf et al. 2026 的核心是比较环境内投影和策略内投影，并分析 action aliasing 与梯度；文中检索不到 `reachable set`、`convex over-approximation` 或 `quadratic program`，不能作为这三个具体设计选择的出处。

**改法**：

> “In the present work, the reachable set is represented by a convex over-approximation and the correction is computed by a quadratic program; these are design choices of this study.”

将 Markgraf et al. 放到其实际支撑的位置：

> “Markgraf et al. (2026) distinguish between environment-integrated and policy-integrated action projection and analyze their different effects on policy-gradient learning.”

---

## 10. CMDP 与 Lagrangian 的技术结论缺少原始出处

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：§2.2：

> “Writing safety as a constrained Markov decision process and solving it with a Lagrangian method guarantees constraint satisfaction in expectation, which again excludes no single command.”

**问题**：这是一项关于约束对象和保证范围的专业结论，但当前仅在上一句引用了一篇安全强化学习综述，没有为 CMDP 的期望累计成本形式提供原始出处；“a Lagrangian method guarantees”也过于笼统，不同算法和假设下的保证并不相同。Altman 是 CMDP 的基础出处，具体强化学习算法应另引相应原始论文。

**改法**：

> “Constrained Markov decision processes are commonly formulated using expected cumulative-cost constraints **(Altman, 1999)**; such a formulation does not, by itself, exclude every individual violating action.”

若必须保留 Lagrangian 方法，另补所实际采用或讨论的 Lagrangian/primal–dual 原始论文，不能只引综述。

---

## 11. 船舶尺寸、动力学限制和时间参数缺少逐项出处

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：§3.1：

> “The subject is a large container vessel of length 175 m and beam 25.4 m. Its limits are \(a_{\max}=0.24\ \mathrm{m/s^2}\), \(\omega_{\max}=0.03\ \mathrm{rad/s}\), and \(v_{\max}=9.5\ \mathrm{m/s}\). The decision period is 10 s, and an episode lasts at most 170 steps...”  
> “The circumscribed radius of the hull is 88.4 m and the inscribed radius is 12.7 m.”

**问题**：175 m、动力学限制、10 s 和 170 步明显对应外部场景/模型参数，但本段没有引用；25.4 m、88.4 m 和 12.7 m 是外部继承还是本文计算，**存疑，需人工核实**。

**改法**：

> “The subject vessel and its dynamic limits follow the container-vessel model used by Krasowski and Althoff (2024, Table II).”

在参数句末加：

> “(Krasowski and Althoff, 2024, Table II)”

对于 25.4 m 和两个半径：

- 若来自外部模型，补精确模型文件、表号或版本；
- 若由本文计算，明确写：  
  > “The circumscribed and inscribed radii are computed from the stated rectangular hull dimensions.”

## 12. 7×7 动作网格及连续动作箱范围缺少出处

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：§3.2：

> “The policy acts in the narrower operating box, whose half-widths are 0.048 m/s² and 0.018 rad/s. These are exactly the span of the 7×7 grid used by the discrete configurations...”

**问题**：数值、网格大小及其与离散基线的对应关系均继承自对比工作的具体配置，但本段没有给出处；后文概括性引用“numerical thresholds follow...”不足以精确覆盖动作网格参数。

**改法**：

> “The operating box reproduces the span of the \(7\times7\) discrete action grid reported by Krasowski and Althoff (2024, Table II), with half-widths of \(0.048\ \mathrm{m/s^2}\) and \(0.018\ \mathrm{rad/s}\).”

## 13. 指称原文“符号与物理含义冲突”却没有给出可核查位置

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：§4.3.1：

> “One clause of the emergency-release test is read by its physical meaning rather than its literal sign, since the two disagree.”

**问题**：该句实质上指称被引工作或代码存在符号错误，但未注明公式号、算法号、代码文件、版本或提交记录，第三方无法核实；该冲突是否确实存在，**存疑，需人工核实**。

**改法**：

> “In [Equation/Algorithm X] of Krasowski and Althoff (2024) [or in file X, commit Y], the printed sign is inconsistent with the accompanying physical description; the present implementation follows the description.”

若无法给出精确位置和版本，删除“since the two disagree”，只陈述本文采用的解释。

## 14. “常见实现会用未裁剪动作计算梯度”属于无来源的软件行为断言

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：§4.3.2：

> “First, common implementations feed the clipped action to the environment and the unclipped action to the policy gradient.”

**问题**：这是版本和代码路径相关的实现事实，不能用 `common implementations` 概括而不引用；Stable-Baselines3 2.3.2 是否完全按照该句所述处理，**存疑，需人工核实**。

**改法**：核对实际代码后限定到具体软件和版本：

> “In Stable-Baselines3 2.3.2, the action passed to the environment is clipped, whereas the policy log-probability is computed from the sampled action [versioned source-code citation].”

引用应指向固定版本的代码文件、行号或永久提交，而不是滚动更新的网页。

## 15. sb3-contrib 及“默认超参数”没有版本化软件引用

**严重度：必改**

**位置**：§4.3.4：

> “Apart from the entropy coefficient, which is set to 0.01, and the bounded action distribution, every hyperparameter takes the default of the implementation library and is left untuned. The libraries are Stable-Baselines3 2.3.2 (Raffin et al. 2021) and sb3-contrib 2.3.0...”

**问题**：Raffin et al. 2021 只能支撑 Stable-Baselines3 项目，不能同时支撑 sb3-contrib 2.3.0；“default”又是版本相关事实，目前没有指向 2.3.2/2.3.0 文档或固定代码版本的引用。

**改法**：

> “The libraries are Stable-Baselines3 2.3.2 **(Raffin et al., 2021)** and sb3-contrib 2.3.0 **[official versioned software citation]**. Default hyperparameters refer specifically to the documentation and source code of versions 2.3.2 and 2.3.0.”

在参考文献表增加 sb3-contrib 2.3.0 的正式软件版本条目，并注明版本或永久存档标识。

## 22. DOI、arXiv 与正式版本的保留规则不统一

**严重度：建议改**

> “For formally published works, cite the final journal or proceedings version and include the DOI consistently. Retain an arXiv identifier only for works that remain preprints, or list it consistently as a secondary identifier after the final publication details.”
