# 论文定位与写作风格核查问题清单

以下问题均依据上传稿《801paper英文版_预览(3).pdf》逐项核查。

## 一、标题与结构化摘要

### 1. `必改`
- **位置**：标题，第 1 页；“**Half-Plane Shielding: Provable Directional COLREGs Compliance for Continuous-Action Safe Reinforcement Learning**”
- **问题**：“Provable”没有在标题中带出正文的关键限定——仅覆盖状态机判定正确、投影可行且未进入 emergency/fallback 的 give-way steps，标题口径大于实际证明范围。
- **改法**：建议删去无条件的 “Provable”，改为：  
  **“Half-Plane Shielding for Per-Step Directional COLREGs Compliance in Continuous-Action Safe Reinforcement Learning”**

### 2. `必改`
- **位置**：摘要—Objectives，第 2 页；“**This paper builds a per-step guarantee of directional compliance for continuous control.**”
- **问题**：未写 “projection is feasible” 和 emergency/fallback exclusions，读起来像所有连续控制步骤均有保证。
- **改法**：  
  **“This paper establishes per-step directional compliance for continuous-control give-way steps on which the projection is feasible.”**

### 3. `建议改`
- **位置**：摘要—Methods，第 2 页；“**A small quadratic program projects the command of the policy onto the nearest point of that set.**”
- **问题**：“small”是无尺度的评价词；正文已有变量数和约束数，可以直接量化。
- **改法**：  
  **“A quadratic program with two variables and at most six linear constraints projects the policy command onto the nearest point of that set.”**

### 4. `必改`
- **位置**：摘要—Findings，第 2 页；“**The ablation gains are significant under a paired signed-rank test.**”
- **问题**：“gains”没有说明是哪些指标、由哪个机制产生，属于无具体对象的摘要式空话。
- **改法**：  
  **“In the ablation, the bounded action distribution reduces the median turn increment and violation count, with paired signed-rank \(p=0.008\) for both comparisons.”**

### 5. `建议改`
- **位置**：摘要—Novelty，第 2 页；“**The paper also states what is proved and what is not.**”
- **问题**：这是行文安排，不是 novelty；也没有说明具体边界，说了等于没说。
- **改法**：删除该句，或改为具体范围陈述：  
  **“The guarantee is explicitly limited to feasible, non-emergency give-way steps.”**

### 6. `必改`
- **位置**：摘要—Practical Applications，第 2 页；“**It can sit above an existing controller as a compliance layer for functional verification and liability assessment. The online cost is one small quadratic program per step, which runs in real time without special hardware.**”
- **问题**：“liability assessment”“without special hardware”以及对现有控制器的直接部署均超出文中仿真实验和单机计时能够支撑的范围；“small”仍然空泛。
- **改法**：  
  **“The shield can be implemented as a controller-independent compliance filter. On the tested CPU, the two-variable quadratic program had a median solve time of 4.11 ms and a 99th-percentile solve time of 8.85 ms, compared with a 10 s decision period.”**

## 二、引言中的独立定位和对标措辞

### 7. `建议改`
- **位置**：第 1 节，第 3 页；“**Two vessels meet at sea. Each turns to starboard, and they pass clear of one another. What makes this work is not the judgment of either bridge team.**”以及“**One property of that rule set decides the shape of the problem.**”
- **问题**：开头采用故事化、断句式和“讲道理式”叙述；“decides the shape of the problem”是抽象修辞，不是具体技术陈述。
- **改法**：局部压缩为：  
  **“Predictable collision avoidance requires autonomous vessels to execute the directional maneuvers prescribed by COLREGs, rather than merely avoid contact.”**

### 8. `必改`
- **位置**：第 1 节，第 3 页；“**Existing work follows two routes, and each gives up one property.**”
- **问题**：把相关工作预设为“各自牺牲一项属性”，是缺陷式、二元对立式定位，不符合“差别写成取舍”的方针。
- **改法**：  
  **“Two common formulations emphasize different properties.”**

### 9. `必改`
- **位置**：第 1 节，第 3 页；“**The first discretizes the action space and masks the non-compliant commands (Krasowski and Althoff 2024).**”
- **问题**：此处是在引言概述一类文献，不是对本文实际借用内容的出处归属；按既定方针，对标工作的详细引用应留在相关工作和实际借用位置。
- **改法**：删去此处括号引用，改为：  
  **“The first discretizes the action space and masks non-compliant commands; Section 2 reviews the representative implementation.”**

### 10. `必改`
- **位置**：第 1 节，第 3 页；“**The price is continuous control. Quantization fixes the control resolution at the grid spacing. A give-way turn that must be readily apparent has a smallest admissible turn rate, and no finite grid lands on it, so every compliant turn overshoots.**”以及“**Control resolution is preserved and the guarantee is lost.**”
- **问题**：“The price is...”“guarantee is lost”把差别写成损失；“no finite grid”“every compliant turn”又是没有限定具体网格的绝对判断。
- **改法**：  
  **“Discrete masking provides command-level exclusion on a finite action set, with control resolution fixed by the grid spacing. For the \(7\times7\) grid evaluated here, the smallest available compliant turn rate exceeds the specified threshold by 37.5%. Continuous reward-based formulations retain continuous resolution but do not impose per-command exclusion as a hard constraint.”**

### 11. `必改`
- **位置**：第 1 节，第 3 页；“**The intersection of continuous action spaces, provable directional compliance and COLREGs has therefore been empty. This paper fills it with a projection-based safety shield. A rule state machine returns the half-plane of compliant directions. That half-plane is intersected with the action box and with a one-step collision-free condition, which gives a convex safe control set. A quadratic program projects the continuous command of the policy onto the nearest point of that set. The step that makes this possible is a change of premise.**”
- **问题**：“has been empty”“fills it”“change of premise”具有宣言式和抢占式色彩；没有限定检索范围，也容易让对标作者感到其工作被用作“空缺背景板”。
- **改法**：  
  **“This paper formulates directional COLREGs compliance as a convex constraint for continuous actions. Replacing discrete enumeration with projection allows the policy command to be corrected within the continuous control space.”**

- **改法**：  
  **“Continuous-action projection has also been studied in safe reinforcement learning (Kochdumper et al. 2023; Markgraf et al. 2026), whereas Müller et al. (2026) use continuous control with reward-based maritime compliance.”**

### 15. `建议改`
- **位置**：第 2.2 节及表 1，第 4–5 页；“**Table 1 places this work on four dimensions and shows where the gap is. A hard guarantee of rule compliance in a maritime setting had been demonstrated only on a discrete action space...**”；表中使用“**Ours**”。

- **改法**：  
  **“Among the maritime studies summarized in Table 1, per-step directional compliance is represented on a discrete action space by Krasowski and Althoff (2024). The present work evaluates a continuous-action projection formulation.”**

### 18. `必改`

## 定位是否清晰

**否。** 核心独立方法能够辨认，但正文多次使用 “has the same form / what is added / follow / taken from / listed as future work” 等句式，把论文写成若干既有工作的拼接式延伸。  
对 Krasowski and Althoff 的真实借用必须保留，但目前场景库、状态机阈值、奖励结构和致谢中的归属没有清楚区分“借用组件”与“本文核心方法”。  
同时，“gap / empty / fills / defects / only / weakest / buys”等措辞把技术取舍写成缺陷或胜负，使标题、摘要和讨论的口径大于正文实际保证范围。
