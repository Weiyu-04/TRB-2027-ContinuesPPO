# 英文论文纯语言层面核查问题清单

核查对象：上传的 20 页英文稿。

# A 组：AI 生成痕迹

### A1
**位置**：1 Introduction，开篇：“**Two vessels meet at sea. Each turns to starboard, and they pass clear of one another. What makes this work is not the judgment of either bridge team.**”

**问题**：电影化场景开头加抽象设问，像自动生成的叙事钩子，不像交通工程论文的直接问题陈述。

**改法**：改为直接陈述，例如：  
“**COLREGs coordinate vessel maneuvers by making each vessel’s expected action predictable.**”

**严重度**：`建议改`

### A2
**位置**：1 Introduction：“**Existing work follows two routes, and each gives up one property.**”

**问题**：“two routes + each gives up one property”是典型的二元对立模板，概括过度整齐。

**改法**：改为：  
“**Existing methods use either discrete action masking or continuous-control reward penalties, and the two approaches retain different properties.**”

**严重度**：`建议改`

### A3
**位置**：1 Introduction：“**The intersection of continuous action spaces, provable directional compliance and COLREGs has therefore been empty. This paper fills it with a projection-based safety shield.**”

**问题**：“intersection has been empty / fills it”是刻意制造记忆点的隐喻，AI 味明显；“This paper”还违反术语锁定。

**改法**：改为：  
“**Ours combines continuous action spaces with per-step directional COLREGs compliance through a safety shield.**”

**严重度**：`建议改`

### A4
**位置**：1 Introduction：“**The step that makes this possible is a change of premise. Action masking needs the safe set to be enumerable. Projection needs it only to be convex.**”

**问题**：三个短句构成过度工整的“转折句 + A needs X + B needs Y”模板。

**改法**：合为直接陈述：  
“**Projection replaces enumeration because it requires a convex safe control set rather than an enumerable one.**”

**严重度**：`建议改`

### A5
**位置**：1 Introduction：“**The second concern of this paper is the scope of what is proved.**”

**问题**：机械式章节导航，且没有提供新的实质信息。

**改法**：删除该句，直接接：  
“**Each guarantee is stated with its assumptions and excluded cases.**”

**严重度**：`建议改`

### A6
**位置**：1 Introduction，Contributions：“**1. A projection-based COLREGs shield… 2. A graded safety statement… 3. An experimental design…**”

**问题**：三项均采用“名词短语标题 + 数个同长度短句 + 收束句”的完全对称结构，模板感较强；第 1、2 项还重复出现“holds by construction”“assumptions”“uncovered cases”等表达。

**改法**：保留三项编号，但删除第 1、2 项中与前文重复的收束句；每项只保留一个贡献对象和一个作用范围。

**严重度**：`可选`

### A7
**位置**：3.1 Vessel Kinematics：“**Two reasons fix this level of detail.**”

**问题**：“Two reasons…”是生成式文本常用的预告句，且“fix this level of detail”搭配生硬。

**改法**：直接写实质内容，例如：  
“**This model keeps the COLREGs constraints directly in the action space.**”

**严重度**：`建议改`

### A8
**位置**：4.2.1 One-Step Collision-Free Constraint：“**We replace it by a convex subset of itself, built in three steps. First… Second… Third…**”

**问题**：全文已经多次使用“两个问题”“三个结果”“三条路线”等结构，此处再次出现标准三步模板，累积后机器感明显。

**改法**：将预告句改为：  
“**A conservative convex subset is constructed as follows.**”  
后续三段不再保留机械的 “First / Second / Third”。

**严重度**：`可选`

### A9
**位置**：Abstract, Novelty：“**The paper also states what is proved and what is not.**”

**问题**：空洞元话语，没有说明具体对象或范围，可整句删除而不损失信息。

**改法**：删除。

**严重度**：`必改`

### A10
**位置**：5.3 Main Comparison：“**Three readings follow from Table 3. First… Second… Third…**”

**问题**：典型“总—分三点”生成模板；后文三个结果本身已足以组织段落。

**改法**：删除 “Three readings follow from Table 3.” 和 “First / Second / Third”，直接逐项报告结果。

**严重度**：`建议改`

### A11
**位置**：5.5 Training Reliability and Sample Efficiency：“**It answers two questions that do not need the re-evaluation, namely whether the shield costs learning speed, and how many seeds of each configuration reach a working policy. First… Second…**”

**问题**：再次使用“answers two questions + First + Second”的模板化框架。

**改法**：改为：  
“**Figure 4 reports learning speed and the number of converged seeds for each configuration.**”

**严重度**：`建议改`

### A12
**位置**：5.5 Training Reliability and Sample Efficiency：“**Panels (c) and (d) give what is bought.**”

**问题**：“give what is bought”是刻意对仗的隐喻，既不自然也没有明确对象。

**改法**：改为：  
“**Panels (c) and (d) report the corresponding collision rates and violations per episode.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### A13
**位置**：5.7 Comparison with External Baselines：“**The question here is not whether a learning method is stronger. It is which dimensions a geometric method already covers.**”

**问题**：使用自问自答式转场，带有演讲稿和机器生成文本的味道。

**改法**：改为：  
“**The comparison identifies the dimensions already covered by geometric controllers.**”

**严重度**：`建议改`

### A14
**位置**：6.2 What Projection Buys and What It Costs：“**Both sides of the exchange were measured directly… The gain is… The cost is…**”

**问题**：“exchange / gain / cost”形成过度工整的三段对称结构，与引言中的“two routes”重复。

**改法**：改用中性主题句：  
“**This section compares control resolution and computation time.**”

**严重度**：`建议改`

### A15
**位置**：7 Conclusions：“**Existing work either buys a provable form of compliance with a discrete action space and masking, or buys control resolution with continuous commands and a soft reward.**”

**问题**：“either buys… or buys…”过度对仗且偏口语；还是对引言二元框架的重复。

**改法**：改为：  
“**Existing methods provide either hard compliance constraints in a discrete action space or continuous control with reward-based compliance.**”

**严重度**：`建议改`

### A16
**位置**：7 Conclusions：“**Three lines of work follow.**”

**问题**：典型的三点式预告句，没有独立信息。

**改法**：删除，或改为：  
“**Future work will address closed-loop fallback verification, multiple target vessels, and a constant-time far-field check.**”

**严重度**：`建议改`

### A17
**位置**：6.3：“**For type approval and accident review this is the more workable form.**”；同节：“**deployment on current shipboard platforms is realistic.**”

**问题**：“more workable”“realistic”均是没有判据、没有作用域的空泛评价词。

**改法**：分别改为：  
“**This form allows reviewers to inspect each classification and projection step without reproducing the training run.**”  
“**The measured computation time is below the 10 s decision period on the stated hardware.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### A18
**位置**：Figure 1：“**Actor–Critic Update**”

**问题**：使用 en dash 连接复合词；本文规定 `–` 只能用于数值区间。

**改法**：改为：  
“**Actor-Critic Update**”  
或面向交通读者改成：  
“**Policy and Value Update**”

**严重度**：`必改`

# B 组：句法与句子结构

按正文、图注和表注统计，排除参考文献、公式及表格数据，小数和连字符词按一个词计：**句长中位数约为 13 词；约 81.3% 的句子不超过 20 词，未达到 95% 的目标。**

### B1
**位置**：1 Introduction：“**It is a set of rules that both sides follow, and that both sides know the other will follow.**”

**问题**：一句中连续出现两个 `that` 从句，第二个从句内部又嵌套宾语结构。

**改法**：  
“**It is a shared rule set. Both bridge teams follow it and expect the other team to do the same.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B2
**位置**：1 Introduction：“**The price is continuous control.**”

**问题**：英语含义实际变成“代价是连续控制”，与上下文需要表达的“失去连续控制”不同；属于搭配错误。

**改法**：  
“**The cost is the loss of continuous control.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B3
**位置**：2.1：“**Most of that work discretizes the command, into a finite set of rudder angles…**”

**问题**：`discretizes the command` 与 `into` 之间不应有逗号。

**改法**：  
“**Most of that work discretizes the control command into a finite set of rudder angles…**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B4
**位置**：2.1：“**Deep reinforcement learning moved the policy into the data.**”

**问题**：“move the policy into the data”不是自然英语搭配，隐喻成分过重。

**改法**：  
“**Deep reinforcement learning learns the policy from data.**”

**严重度**：`建议改`

### B5
**位置**：3.2：“**That solves the dimension only.**”

**问题**：表达不完整且不地道，`solve the dimension`不是正常搭配。

**改法**：  
“**This keeps the observation dimension fixed but does not address multiple target vessels.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B6
**位置**：4 Methodology：“**A note on the guarantees.**”

**问题**：句子残缺，只是一个未排版为标题的名词短语。

**改法**：删除；下一句直接开始。若保留为小标题，应改成正式标题格式：  
“**Scope of the Guarantees**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B7
**位置**：4.1：“**The first part separates “about to hold” from “already holds”…**”

**问题**：`hold`缺少明确主语或宾语；“about to hold / already holds”语法形式也不平行。

**改法**：  
“**The first part distinguishes an obligation that is about to apply from one that already applies.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B8
**位置**：4.1：“**The relative speed must also close the current center distance in time.**”

**问题**：`speed closes a distance`搭配不自然，`in time`指向不明。

**改法**：  
“**The relative motion must also reduce the center distance within the check horizon.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B9
**位置**：4.2.1：“**The margin inside the safety distance is what does.**”

**问题**：`does`没有可恢复的明确宾语或动作，属于不完整指代。

**改法**：  
“**The added margin provides the conservative buffer.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B10
**位置**：4.2.1, Assumptions：“**That bound is not implied by the dynamics and is stated separately; the largest value measured over the 2,000 scenarios of the library is 7.10 m/s.**”

**问题**：用分号硬连两个可独立成立的句子，违反“一句一事”。

**改法**：  
“**That bound is not implied by the dynamics and is stated separately. The largest value measured in the 2,000-scenario library is 7.10 m/s.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B11
**位置**：4.2.3, Emergency branch：  
“**A target vessel astern that acceleration can clear takes a straight-line escape inside the reaction window.**”  
“**One position-tracking law turns all three into a control command.**”

**问题**：第一句的语法主语变成 `A target vessel`，导致目标船“takes a straight-line escape”；第二句中 `turns all three into a control command`的逻辑宾语不清。

**改法**：  
“**If acceleration can clear a target vessel astern, the emergency controller follows a straight-line escape trajectory within the reaction window. The same position-tracking law converts each of the three modes into a control command.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B12
**位置**：4.2.3, Other situations：“**Otherwise the direction constraint is relaxed, and the collision risk is minimized if that also fails.**”

**问题**：`that`的先行内容不明确；一个句子同时写放松约束和第二层失败处理。

**改法**：  
“**Otherwise, the direction constraint is relaxed. If no feasible control command is then found, the fallback minimizes collision risk.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B13
**位置**：4.3：“**The policy learns on the transition it will actually meet…**”

**问题**：`learns on the transition`和`meet a transition`都不是自然搭配。

**改法**：  
“**The policy is trained on the transition that it will experience during deployment…**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B14
**位置**：4.3.2：“**The standard deviation therefore has a one-way channel to grow…**”；“**both channels close…**”

**问题**：以“channel”描述方差变化机制，前后指代抽象，读者必须回推“两条通道”具体指什么。

**改法**：改成字面陈述，例如：  
“**The standard deviation can therefore continue to increase without improving the reward. The bounded distribution removes both effects.**”

**严重度**：`建议改`

### B15
**位置**：4.3.4：“**every hyperparameter takes the default of the implementation library**”

**问题**：`takes the default`搭配生硬，且 `the default`暗示所有参数共用一个默认值。

**改法**：  
“**every other hyperparameter uses its default value from the implementation library**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B16
**位置**：5.1：“**the CommonOcean benchmark … which holds 2,000 scenarios**”；“**It ends with a goal region…**”

**问题**：数据集通常用 `contains`，不用 `holds`；场景也不宜表述为“ends with a goal region”。

**改法**：  
“**the CommonOcean benchmark, which contains 2,000 scenarios**”  
“**Each scenario includes a goal region with a position gate, a heading gate, and a time limit.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B17
**位置**：Table 2 Notes：“**A check mark is on, a cross is off, and a dash is not applicable.**”；“**Those three steps can only be attributed as a whole.**”

**问题**：`is on / is off`口语化；`steps can be attributed`缺少被归因的结果对象。

**改法**：  
“**A check mark denotes enabled, a cross denotes disabled, and a dash denotes not applicable.**”  
“**The effects of these three changes can only be interpreted jointly with their companion variables.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B18
**位置**：5.1：“**the step from the discrete baseline to that configuration can be negative**”

**问题**：`step can be negative`指代不明；实际是某项差值或性能变化为负。

**改法**：  
“**the measured difference between the discrete baseline and that configuration can be negative**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B19
**位置**：5.2, Training budget：“**Machine time did not allow the same during training.**”

**问题**：`the same`的具体指代距离过远，可能指同机评估、同机训练或单次运行。

**改法**：  
“**Available machine time did not permit all training runs to be completed on the same machine.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B20
**位置**：5.2, Checkpoint selection：“**The candidates are those twenty checkpoints, the criterion is the highest arrival rate on the validation set, ties go to the earlier checkpoint, and the test set is never touched.**”

**问题**：一句并列四件独立事情，虽然未超过 30 词，但明显过载。

**改法**：  
“**The twenty saved checkpoints are the candidates. The checkpoint with the highest arrival rate on the validation set is selected. Ties go to the earlier checkpoint. The test set is not accessed.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B21
**位置**：Table 3 后正文：“**a diverged seed pollutes the per-episode averages and the direction of that pollution differs by configuration.**”

**问题**：`seed pollutes averages`和`direction of pollution`均为不必要的拟人化隐喻。

**改法**：  
“**a nonconverged run shifts the per-episode averages, and the direction of the shift differs among configurations.**”

**严重度**：`必改`

### B22

**问题**：图中宽的是 `error bars`，不是一般的 `bars`；`contain a diverged seed`也不准确。

**问题**：`Neither`可能指两个变化、两个结果或两个百分比，先行对象不清。

# C 组：术语一致性

“**underactuated vessels**”
