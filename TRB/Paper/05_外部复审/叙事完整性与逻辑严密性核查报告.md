## A. 偏离主线、可整段删除或迁移的内容

### 1. `可选`
- **位置**：§2.2，原句：“**A directional constraint tightens the feasible set in close quarters and can cost terminal efficiency. Potential-based shaping recovers it...**”
- **问题**：该段连续讨论 potential shaping、goal stalling、sample efficiency 和 asymptotic optimality，未参与“方向性约束→半平面→凸投影→分级保证”的核心缺口论证；删除后，Table 1 到问题定义的逻辑仍完整。
- **改法**：删除整段；或仅在 §4.3.1 保留一句：`Potential-based shaping is included only as an experimental refinement and is not part of the compliance guarantee.`

### 2. `可选`
- **位置**：§3.1，原句：“**Two reasons fix this level of detail.**”至“**Wind, current, and waves are not modeled.**”
- **问题**：该段主要为简化动力学模型辩护，不推进动作平面半平面和投影的论证；末句属于局限，其余内容可删而不损伤主线。
- **改法**：删除前面的模型辩护，只把 `Wind, current, and waves are not modeled.` 移入局限段。

### 3. `必改`
- **位置**：§6.3，原句：“**For type approval and accident review this is the more workable form.**”；“**deployment on current shipboard platforms is realistic.**”
- **问题**：整节从技术机制跳到型式认可、事故审查和现役船载部署，但正文没有监管流程、法律责任、船载硬件或现场部署证据；既偏离主线，又构成结论越界。
- **改法**：删除 §6.3；或只保留：`The constraint and projection can be audited step by step, subject to correct state classification and implementation.` 删除 `type approval`、`accident review`、`liability assessment`、`realistic deployment` 等措辞。

## B. 核心论证链条中的自相矛盾或跳步

### 4. `必改`
- **位置**：§1，原句：“**Existing work follows two routes, and each gives up one property.**”
- **问题**：这一总括没有限定为“学习型 COLREGs 方法”，但 §2.1 随即列出第三类 model-driven continuous hard-constraint methods；因此“两条路穷尽既有工作”与相关工作部分自相矛盾。
- **改法**：改为：`Existing learning-based approaches to directional COLREGs compliance mainly follow two routes, and each gives up one of the two properties considered here.`

### 5. `必改`
- **位置**：§1，原句：“**no finite grid lands on it, so every compliant turn overshoots.**”；§6.2，原句：“**The common statement that a finer grid approaches continuous control therefore does not hold for this task.**”
- **问题**：有限网格在阈值恰好是网格点时可以精确落在阈值上；而且“不单调”不等于“不收敛”，网格间距趋零时最大量化误差仍趋零。文中的 5×5 与 7×7 例子只能证明特定网格间不单调。
- **改法**：改为：`A fixed uniform grid generally does not land exactly on the threshold unless the threshold coincides with a grid point. For the 5×5 and 7×7 grids examined here, the overshoot is non-monotonic.` 删除 `no finite grid` 和 `does not hold for this task`。

### 6. `必改`
- **位置**：摘要 Methods、§1、§4.2.1，原句：“**a one-step collision-free condition, which gives a convex safe control set**”；“**We replace it by a convex subset of itself**”；随后又写：“**A first-order expansion is neither an inner nor an outer approximation. Equation (6) alone therefore does not establish conservatism.**”
- **问题**：前文把 Equation (6) 称为真实无碰集合的凸子集和“collision-free condition”，后文却明确承认它既非内近似也非外近似；“**The margin absorbs the linearization residual**”也没有给出残差上界。Proposition 1 仅证明远场在任意控制下单步不会相撞，并未证明 Equation (6) 的线性约束在近场保守。
- **改法**：统一改称 `a linearized one-step separation constraint` 或 `a convex surrogate constraint`；把 `convex subset` 改成 `convex surrogate`；明确写：`Strict one-step collision freedom is proved only under the far-field condition in Proposition 1.`

### 7. `必改`
- **位置**：Table 1 图注及 Ours 行，原句：“**A per-step hard guarantee means ... at every decision step**”；Ours 的 guarantee 栏写为“**Direction**”。
- **问题**：Proposition 2 只覆盖“状态机正确、give-way、投影可行、非 emergency/fallback”的步，并非 every decision step；表格定义与正文保证范围冲突。
- **改法**：把列名或脚注改为：`Guarantee on covered steps`；Ours 行改为：`Direction on feasible give-way steps`。

### 8. `必改`
- **位置**：§3.3，Equation (2) 前后，原句：“**such that \(u_t\in U_{\mathrm{colregs}}(\rho_t)\cap U_{\mathrm{cf}}(s_t)\) for all \(t\).**”；“**It constrains every command that is executed.**”
- **问题**：实际控制器在 emergency 和空集 fallback 时主动绕过这些约束，因此本文算法并没有求解这里定义的“for all \(t\)”问题。
- **改法**：将问题定义限定为：`for every non-emergency step on which the constrained set is feasible`；另行定义 emergency/fallback 的 uncovered transition。

### 9. `建议改`
- **位置**：§3.3，原句：“**Assumption (A2) is not a simplification adopted for convenience. It is the scope of the convention. The rules are written for a pair of vessels.**”
- **问题**：下一句已经承认三船及以上可以同时相遇并产生冲突；因此“不是简化，而是公约范围”表述过满。正文实际采用的是“成对义务的单目标实现”，而非公约只适用于两船。
- **改法**：改为：`The present formulation applies the pairwise obligations of COLREGs to one controlled vessel and one target vessel. Resolving simultaneous obligations involving multiple targets is outside the present scope.`

### 10. `必改`
- **位置**：§3.3，原句：“**(A5) No give-way obligation is already in force at the initial instant.**”；§4.3.3，原句：“**Suppose an encounter already satisfies a give-way predicate at the initial instant.**”
- **问题**：用于证明 symmetric entry 必要性的 adversarial case 明确违反 A5；正文没有说明方法范围是否已通过该修改扩展到 A5 之外。
- **改法**：二选一：删除 A5；或在 §4.3.3 明确写：`These adversarial cases lie outside Assumption A5 and are used only as an implementation diagnostic, not as evidence within the formal problem scope.`

### 11. `必改`
- **位置**：§4.1 Equation (4) 与 §4.2.2，原句：“**\(\{\omega\le-\omega_{\mathrm{turn}}\}\) or \(\{\omega\ge+\omega_{\mathrm{turn}}\}\), \(\rho=\rho_4\)**”；“**\(U_{\mathrm{safe}}\) ... is a convex polygon**”；“**the fifth is the compliant-direction half-plane**”。
- **问题**：overtaking 的允许集合是两个半平面的并集，不是单个半平面，也不是凸集；因此对 \(\rho_4\) 而言，“三集合交集是一个凸多边形”“一个 QP、最多六条线性约束”的叙述不成立。
- **改法**：按实际实现写清：若实现为两个候选凸子问题，就写 `the overtaking case is handled as two convex subproblems`；若本文没有这种实现，则把 convex-polygon 和单-QP结论限定为 \(\rho_0,\rho_1,\rho_2,\rho_3\)，并明确 overtaking 未进入该保证。

### 12. `必改`
- **位置**：§4.1，原句：“**A give-way predicate must not hold now. It must hold at every decision step of a 60 s reaction window...**”；§4.3.3，原句：“**the immediate condition is checked, and the give-way situation is entered if it already holds.**”
- **问题**：§4.1 描述的是修改前的非对称进入规则，§4.3.3 才给出最终规则；读者在核心半平面构造处得到的并非最终状态机。
- **改法**：在 §4.1 直接写最终 entry rule，包括 persistence 与 immediate 两条路径；§4.3.3 只保留修改动机和消融说明。

### 13. `建议改`
- **位置**：§4.1，原句：“**This is the smallest compliant turn rate. Any turn at least this large complies, and the bound cannot be lowered.**”
- **问题**：0.008727 rad/s 是在本文选定的 20°/40 s 操作化定义下得到的阈值；仅达到幅值并不自动满足方向、状态识别及其他 COLREGs 条件。
- **改法**：改为：`Under the adopted 20°-within-40-s operational definition, this is the minimum turn-rate magnitude. A command also has to satisfy the required direction and the applicable state-machine conditions.`

### 14. `必改`
- **位置**：§4.2.3，原句：“**Its purpose is a defined behavior once no compliant feasible command remains.**”；“**Purpose-built conflict cases exercise the branches, and their outcome is reported with the shield measurements.**”
- **问题**：emergency branch 是按 emergency state 直接触发，并未先证明“无合规可行命令”；此外 §5.6 只报总体分支占比，没有报 purpose-built cases 的分支级结果或 outcome。
- **改法**：第一句改为：`Its purpose is to provide a defined action after the emergency state has been declared.` 第二句若无对应结果则删除；有结果则明确给出表、图或场景编号。

### 15. `必改`
- **位置**：§4.3，原句：“**The policy therefore faces the composed transition \(P(\cdot\mid s_t,\Pi_{U_{\mathrm{safe}}}(u_t))\).**”
- **问题**：实际 executed action 是 projection、emergency controller 和 fallback 的分段函数；Equation (11) 只写投影分支，不能代表完整环境转移。
- **改法**：将 applied action 定义为分段式，例如 `u_exec = u_em`、`u_fb` 或 `Π_Usafe(u_policy)`，再写 `P(·|s_t,u_exec)`；或明确说明 Equation (11) 只适用于可行的非 emergency 步。

### 16. `建议改`
- **位置**：Figure 1 图注与 §4.3.2，原句：“**projects \(u_{\mathrm{policy}}\) onto \(u_{\mathrm{safe}}\)**”；“**Equation (13) gives ... the applied action.**”
- **问题**：投影对象应是集合 \(U_{\mathrm{safe}}\)，结果才是 \(u_{\mathrm{safe}}\)；Beta 输出是 policy command，不是环境最终 applied action。
- **改法**：图注改为：`projects \(u_{\mathrm{policy}}\) onto \(U_{\mathrm{safe}}\), producing \(u_{\mathrm{safe}}\)`；Equation (13) 后改为 `gives the policy command before shielding`。

## C. 实验设计与承诺兑现问题

### 17. `必改`
- **位置**：摘要、§1 Contribution 3、§5.1，原句：“**Nine configurations form a single-variable ladder**”；“**each level changes a single variable from the level before it**”；Table 2 注又写：“**Three steps ... carry a companion variable that cannot be separated.**”
- **问题**：三个 companion variables 已否定“每一步单变量”；而且 Table 2 当前行序中 `Ablation, bounded only → Ablation, symmetric only` 同时关闭 bounded、打开 symmetric，不是单变量递进。
- **改法**：删除 `single-variable ladder` 和 `each level changes a single variable`；改为：`The configurations form a connected comparison graph. Several edges represent inseparable component bundles, as listed in Table 2.`

### 18. `必改`
- **位置**：§5.1，原句：“**The gain attributed to the shield is therefore measured against the weakest form of continuous action... it should be read as a conservative lower bound.**”
- **问题**：使用较弱对照通常可能放大而非必然缩小观察到的增益；“weakest”不能推出“conservative lower bound”。
- **改法**：改为：`The comparison is specific to this plain continuous baseline and should not be interpreted as a general lower or upper bound on the effect of shielding.`

### 19. `必改`
- **位置**：§5.2 Metrics，原句：“**The main metrics are ... the episode duration. Control quality, safety margin ... are also reported, and every metric is broken down by encounter situation.**”
- **问题**：正文没有报告 episode duration；没有独立 safety-margin 汇总；也没有把 every metric 按 encounter situation 分解。
- **改法**：删除未报告项目，并改为：`Selected metrics are additionally broken down by encounter situation.` 若确有数据，则必须给出对应表或图的位置。

### 20. `必改`
- **位置**：§5.2 Statistics 与 §5.4，原句：“**The paired test is a per-seed paired sign test**”；消融部分却写：“**a paired signed-rank value**”。
- **问题**：sign test 和 Wilcoxon signed-rank test 是不同检验；§5.2 的总括使读者以为全文只使用 sign test。
- **改法**：改为：`The main nine-configuration comparisons use a paired sign test. The 2×2 ablation comparisons use a paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test.`

### 21. `必改`
- **位置**：§5.2、§5.3、§5.4、§5.5、结论，原句：“**The best-checkpoint version and the last-checkpoint version are therefore both reported.**”；“**a converged-seeds-only version is also reported**”；“**Per-seed raw values accompany the table.**”
- **问题**：当前 20 页稿中只出现 best-checkpoint、all-seeds 的 Table 3；没有 last-checkpoint 表、converged-only 主表、converged-only 消融表，也没有各指标的 per-seed raw values。Figure 2(b) 只显示 collision per seed，不能兑现这一承诺。
- **改法**：没有篇幅增加结果时，删除所有 `both reported`、`also reported`、`accompany the table`；如这些数据在外部材料中，则明确写文件名和可访问位置。

### 22. `建议改`
- **位置**：§5.2，原句：“**Taking the maximum ... lifts the reported value by about 7.5 points... That is four times the drift of about 1.6 points.**”
- **问题**：7.5 ÷ 1.6 ≈ 4.69，不是 four times。
- **改法**：改为：`about 4.7 times` 或 `nearly five times`。

### 23. `必改`
- **位置**：§5.3，原句：“**That gap is the cost of the shield.**”
- **问题**：前一句把 Ours 同三个 unshielded configurations 和 discrete safe baseline 一并比较；这些配置除 shield 外还存在动作空间、分布、shaping、entry 等差异，不能把整体 arrival gap 单独归因于 shield。
- **改法**：改为：`The lower arrival rate is an observed cost of the full shielded configuration. The isolated shield comparison is the Continuous, no shield versus Continuous, shield pair.`

### 24. `必改`
- **位置**：§5.4，原句：“**Smoothness comes entirely from the bounded action distribution, and both contribute to compliance.**”
- **问题**：bounded distribution 同时改变 initial exploration scale；“entirely”超过当前消融可识别范围。总 violation 指标又混合 stand-on 与 give-way，不能直接证明两个修改分别“贡献于合规”的哪一部分。
- **改法**：改为：`Within this component bundle, the bounded-distribution configuration is associated with lower turn increments. Both refinements are associated with lower aggregate violation counts, but their effects cannot be separated from the declared companion variable or by violation type.`

### 25. `必改`
- **位置**：§5.2 与 §5.4–5.5，原句：“**How often the mismatch occurs was not measured.**”
- **问题**：正文明确承认 offline scorer 的窗口与 shield guarantee 不一致且未测错配频率，随后却用 aggregate violation count 解释各机制“contribute to compliance”；中间缺少“指标如何反映机制”的桥梁。
- **改法**：所有相关结果改称 `empirical trajectory-level violation metric`；明确写：`This metric is not a direct empirical test of Proposition 2 because the counting windows differ.`

## D. 图表、训练集与测试集口径问题

### 26. `必改`

## E. 讨论、结论与局限范围

### 35. `必改`
- **位置**：§6.1，原句：“**Both refinements belong to the continuous action space.**”；“**Both problems appear only once a COLREGs shield is moved into a continuous action space.**”；“**The clearable set of this work suggests one route...**”
- **问题**：bounded distribution 确属连续动作问题，但 symmetric give-way entry 是状态机进入规则，在离散动作系统中同样可能出现；“clearable set”全文未定义。
- **改法**：改为：`The bounded action distribution addresses a continuous-action issue, whereas symmetric entry addresses a state-machine issue.` 删除 `clearable set`，或在方法部分先正式定义。

### 36. `必改`
- **位置**：§6.2，原句：“**Even at \(K=121\), which is 14,641 commands, it stays well below the time of one projection.**”
- **问题**：正文没有给出 enumeration timing、测试硬件、重复次数或对照表；“Both sides ... were measured directly”中的这一侧没有被实际报告。
